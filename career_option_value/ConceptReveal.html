A choice has option value when it preserves valuable choices that can be exercised after uncertainty is resolved.

In the game, Economics did not provide the highest payoff in every labor market. Instead, it kept several relatively valuable career markets accessible. That reduced the risk of graduating into an economy in which very few strong opportunities were available to you.

The basic tradeoff
State-specific upside ↔ Flexibility across uncertain futures

If you know exactly where you are going, specialization can be valuable. If you must invest in human capital before the future is known, keeping good options open can itself be valuable.
